Advanced cooking in 5e: Meals

by Xenoanubis

How meals work

Making a meal is the act of converting raw resources into the equivalent of a day's rations. During a long rest, as long as you have the tools and ingredients, you may attempt to craft a gourmet meal for yourself and up to 5 others. The DC is 15, and the skill used in the attempt is dependent on how you describe your cooking process. Starting out, this is largely fluff, however, if the roll is 20 or over, and the meal doesn’t already have one being applied, there is a random chance the meal will be good enough to grant effects to those consuming them. Any time this happens, you retain that effect as a Recipe, which can be applied to any future meals. Recipes can also be sought out and learned.

Recipes

  • 1. Hearty: The heft of this meal grants 10 temporary hitpoint until your next long rest.
  • 2. Zesty: The flavors of this meal spark a +3 bonus the next time you roll initiative before a long rest.
  • 3. Refreshing: The meals revitalizing flavor grants a Recharge point until your next long rest. You may use this at any point, including combat, to gain the benefits of a short rest, minus rolling hit die.
  • 4. Humble: Until your next long rest, this simple meal grants advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.
  • 5. Invigorating: The vitality boosting quality of this meal grants advantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution saves until your next long rest.
  • 6. Inspired: The surprising nature of this meal grants you inspiration. (This effect cannot be learned as a recipe.)
  • 7. Bracning: This meal grants a Brace point, which can be spent when struck by an attack to halve the damage.
  • 8. Decadent: Knowing this recipe allows you to apply 2 recipe effects to a meal at the cost of increasing the DC to 18.
  • 9. Warming: The heat of this meal grants you resistance to cold and advantage on saves against cold climate related effects until your next long rest.
  • 10. Chilled: The cooling nature of this meal grants resistance to fire and advantage of saves related to hot weather.
  • 11. Light: The airiness of this meal grants advantage on Dexterity skill checks until you next long rest.
  • 12. Savory: The flavor of this meal is empowering, granting advantage of Strength based skill checks until you next long rest.
  • 13. Aromatic: The smell and taste of this meal awakens the senses, granting advantage on Wisdom based skill checks until your next long rest.
  • 14. Heartfelt: The effort and soul put into this meal makes you feel more connected and open, granting advantage on Charisma based skill checks until your next long rest.
  • 15. Exotic: The strangeness of the dish causes one to think, granting advantage on Intelligence based skill checks until your next long rest.
  • 16. Healthy: Loaded with what the body needs, this meal gives advantage on Constitution based skill checks until your next long rest.
  • 17. Filling: This meal fills you up, and because of it the next time you spend hit die to heal, they are all maxed.
  • 18. Energizing: This meal keeps you going, making you ignore the effect of up to 3 levels of exhaustion until your next long or short rest.
  • 19. Nutritious: This meal is full of what the body needs. You have advantage on your next saving throw.
  • 20. Nourishing: This meal has potent health benefits, granting the effects of a lesser restoration spell.
